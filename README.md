# Employee-Leave-Payroll-Management-System
This project uses AI to extract bill details from uploaded receipts, automatically fill expense records, detect duplicates and calculation errors, and help supervisors review and approve employee expenses efficiently.
## Problem Statement

Traditional employee expense management systems require employees to manually enter expense details, calculate bill amounts, and compile multiple receipts into PDFs before submitting them for approval. This process is time-consuming, prone to data-entry errors, and creates additional work for supervisors, who must manually review receipts and verify the entered information.

The proposed system aims to develop an AI-powered Employee Expense and Bill Verification System integrated into an existing Employee Management and Attendance Management System. Employees will be able to upload images of bills and receipts directly to the platform. The AI will automatically extract relevant information, such as merchant name, bill date, expense amount, taxes, and expense category, and populate the expense table. The system will also perform preliminary validation checks to identify missing information, calculation discrepancies, and potential duplicate bills. Supervisors will be able to view the original receipt alongside the extracted details and approve, reject, or request corrections.

The solution aims to reduce manual effort, improve data accuracy, streamline expense processing, and make employee reimbursement management more efficient and transparent.
